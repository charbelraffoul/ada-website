  <h1 class="ds-h1">Oil shocks, sectors, and portfolio design</h1>

  <p class="ds-lead">
    Oil price shocks rarely hit markets uniformly. Some sectors react immediately through costs and margins,
    others respond through demand and risk sentiment, and a few behave like stabilizers.
    This story quantifies those differences and turns them into portfolio-relevant insights.
  </p>

  <div class="ds-divider"></div>

  <div class="ds-grid">
    <div class="ds-card span-6">
      <span class="ds-tag">Idea</span>
      <h3>One shock, many reactions</h3>
      <p>
        We treat oil shocks as “stress events” and measure how sector returns behave around them.
        The goal is to identify which parts of the market are most exposed versus most resilient.
      </p>
    </div>

    <div class="ds-card span-6">
      <span class="ds-tag">Why it matters</span>
      <h3>From explanation to allocation</h3>
      <p>
        If the impact differs by sector and environment, portfolio construction should reflect that.
        We evaluate cyclical vs defensive allocations and highlight the trade-offs.
      </p>
    </div>

  </div>
